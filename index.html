Google Authenticator Export Converter

Turn a Google Authenticator Transfer accounts export into standard otpauth:// URIs and one QR code per account, ready to import into any other authenticator or password manager.

Everything runs in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged — the page is blocked from making network requests at all.

1. Add your export

In Google Authenticator: menu → Transfer accounts → Export accounts. If the export spans several QR codes, add each one.

Choose QR code images or drop them here, or paste a screenshot (Ctrl/⌘+V)

2. Your accounts

These QR codes and URIs contain your secret keys. Anyone who sees them can generate your codes — keep them off shared screens and don't store them unencrypted.

    Works offline. QR decoding by jsQR, QR generation by qrcode-generator.
